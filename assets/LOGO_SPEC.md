# LOGO_SPEC.md — Startup Logo Asset Specification

- **Dimensions**: `512 x 512 px` (`1:1` square, rounded corners rx=120px for iOS/App Icon style)
- **Vector Reference**: `assets/logo.svg`
- **Primary Colors**: Royal Blue `#4F8DFF`, Neon Violet `#8B5CF6`, Deep Obsidian `#09090B`
- **Design Rules**:
  - Must look crisp and recognizable when scaled down to 16x16 px Chrome Extension favicon.
  - No text inside the icon symbol.
- **Midjourney Prompt**:
  > *"Minimalist 3D glassmorphism icon of an electric lightning bolt integrated into a sleek browser side panel frame. Vibrant gradient from royal blue (#4F8DFF) to neon violet (#8B5CF6) on a dark obsidian background (#09090B). Apple App Store icon quality, crisp edges --ar 1:1"*
